Privacy & statistics

When you visit, you automatically get a random, anonymous user ID (no name, no e-mail). Stored are your feedback and usage statistics: page views, time on the page and listening time per song (to the nearest 10 seconds), plus device type, browser, engine version and the result of a speed test. These tables contain no IP address. If you register with an e-mail address, it is stored as well. The data is hosted at Supabase (database provider). The counters under the genre switcher show only totals of the last 30 days. Statistics can be switched off in the account panel (top right) and stay off if your browser sends “Do Not Track”.
